The scenario

Most of the spacecraft and its equipment were destroyed or damaged during the landing. 15 items remain intact and undamaged. Your survival depends on reaching the mother ship, so you must decide which supplies are most important for the journey.

Assume that:

  • The crew is wearing space suits, which provide oxygen and protection from the lunar environment.
  • The crew must travel approximately 200 miles to reach the rendezvous point.
  • The listed supplies are the items that survived the crash.
